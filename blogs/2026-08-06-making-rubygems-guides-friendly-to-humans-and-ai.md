The RubyGems guides at guides.rubygems.org are read by more than humans these days. AI agents fetch them to answer questions about building, publishing, and installing gems. Evil Martians’ Ruby/Rails LLM discoverability scorecard asks how easily an agent can find and read Ruby documentation, and the guides had no good answer.
